VOLLEYWHEEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

VolleyWheel is a rotation board for volleyball coaches. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. VolleyWheel does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server.

  • No account. There is no sign-up, login, or profile.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No network connection. The app does not send your data anywhere. It has no backend.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in VolleyWheel — your teams, rosters, player names, colors, positions, saved lineups, and settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone (a file named teams.json in the app's Documents folder).

  • This data is included in your device's normal iCloud or Finder backup, if you have backups enabled. That backup is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by VolleyWheel.
  • If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.
  • We have no ability to access, recover, or view this data.

Purchases

If you buy the “Unlock Unlimited” option, that purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; VolleyWheel never sees your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

VolleyWheel does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “last updated” date.
