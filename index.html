Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. VolleyWheel has no account and no server — your teams, rosters, and lineups never leave your device. They're included in your normal iCloud or Finder device backup if you have backups on.
I got a new phone / reinstalled the app and my teams are gone.
Data lives in the app's local storage, so it's removed if the app is deleted. Restoring your phone from a device backup that included VolleyWheel brings it back; otherwise it can't be recovered.
What does the paid unlock include?
The free version covers one team with up to three saved lineups. A single “Unlock Unlimited” purchase (one time, no subscription) removes both limits. Use Restore Purchases in the ••• menu after reinstalling or on a new device.
How do I rotate / follow the serve order?
Spin the wheel with your finger, or tap Rotate Left / Rotate Right. The number in the center of the wheel is the current rotation (1–6). Turn on Zone Numbers in the ••• menu to show positions 1–6 on the court.
How do I keep a different lineup for each set?
Your wheel is always saved to the active lineup automatically. Tap + in the lineup bar for a fresh one, and tap a lineup's name to switch, rename, duplicate, or delete it.
